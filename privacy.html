Concit

Privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026

Concit helps friends and family choose where to go out in Milan. This notice explains what personal data the app keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

Who runs Concit

Concit is run by Cristiano Nocivelli, who is responsible for your data. Contact: conclude.it@outlook.com.

What Concit keeps

  • Guests: if you vote from a plan link without creating an account, Concit keeps only the name you type, your votes and the plans you joined, tied to that device. Guests see only the plan they were invited to. You can turn it into a full account or delete it by tapping your initial.
  • Your account: your name, your email address, your password and, if you add one, a profile picture. The app crops it to a square and saves a small copy and a larger one (up to 1080 pixels wide), and only people in your groups can see it. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody can read it.
  • Your groups: the groups you create or join, and who is in them.
  • Your messages: what you write in a group chat, the photos you send there and your reactions to messages, with the time you sent them. Photos are shrunk on your device before they are sent. Only members of that group can see any of this.
  • Online, typing and read: the time the app last checked in while you had it open, and whether you are typing in a group chat. Members of your groups see "online" if you used the app in the last minute, and "typing…" while you write. When you open a group chat, the other members can see which of their messages you have read.
  • Your plans and votes: the plans you start or join, the places suggested, how you voted, and any meeting point you drop on the map. People in the plan can see that point.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on: the address your phone or browser gives Concit for sending it notifications, linked to your account. It is removed when you turn notifications off, log out or delete your account. Notifications say who started a plan, the group name and what the plan is. Chat notifications show the sender's name and the start of the message.
  • How the app is used: Concit records when you open the app, which screens you look at, and taps on the booking, directions and share buttons, linked to your account. It is used only to see what works and improve the app, it is kept in Concit's own database with no outside analytics company, and it is unlinked from you if you delete your account.
  • On your own device: your login session and the events you save are kept in your browser's storage. Concit uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

Other members of your groups and plans can see your name and how far you are with voting. Concit does not show your email address to anyone.

Why

Concit uses this data only to run the app for you: to log you in, run your groups, plans and group chats, and show results. The legal basis is that it is needed to provide the service you signed up for (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR). Your data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone except the services below.

Services Concit relies on

  • Supabase stores accounts and app data and sends login emails. The database is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your device, deliver notifications if you turn them on. The message is encrypted so that only your device can read it.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that logins, sign-ups and guest votes come from a person and not a bot. It runs on the logged-out screens and sees your IP address and basic browser details.
  • Vercel hosts the app's web pages and sends the notifications. Like any web host, it handles your IP address when you open the app.
  • Wikimedia Commons supplies the photos on the Events tab. Your browser loads them directly, so Wikimedia sees your IP address.
  • OpenFreeMap and jsDelivr supply the map and map software. Your browser contacts them directly when you open the map, so they see your IP address.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU, they rely on the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile (including your picture), votes, messages and group memberships are deleted straight away, and you are removed from every plan. Plans you started that others are still in stay for them, without your name. Copies in the hosting providers' backups disappear as those backups expire.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, or object to how it is used. You can change your name and delete your account yourself under Groups, then Account and privacy. For anything else, email the address above.

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority where you live.

Age

You need to be at least 14 to create a Concit account.

Changes
