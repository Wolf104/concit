Concit

Privacy notice

Last updated 10 November 2026

Concit helps friends and family choose where to go out in Milan. This notice explains what personal data the app keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

Who runs Concit

Concit is run by Cristiano Nocivelli, who is responsible for your data. Contact: cnocivelli06@gmail.com.

What Concit keeps

  • Your account: your name, your email address and your password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody can read it.
  • Your groups: the groups you create or join, and who is in them.
  • Your plans and votes: the plans you start or join, the places suggested, how you voted, and any meeting point you drop on the map. People in the plan can see that point.
  • On your own device: your login session and the events you save are kept in your browser's storage. Concit uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

Other members of your groups and plans can see your name and how far you are with voting. Concit does not show your email address to anyone.

Why

Concit uses this data only to run the app for you: to log you in, run your groups and plans, and show results. The legal basis is that it is needed to provide the service you signed up for (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR). Your data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone except the services below.

Services Concit relies on

  • Supabase stores accounts and app data and sends login emails. The database is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Vercel hosts the app's web pages. Like any web host, it handles your IP address when you open the app.
  • OpenFreeMap and jsDelivr supply the map and map software. Your browser contacts them directly when you open the map, so they see your IP address.
  • Wikimedia Commons supplies the place photos. Your browser loads them directly, so Wikimedia sees your IP address.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU, they rely on the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, votes and group memberships are deleted straight away, and you are removed from every plan. Plans you started that others are still in stay for them, without your name. Copies in the hosting providers' backups disappear as those backups expire.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, or object to how it is used. You can change your name and delete your account yourself under Groups, then Account and privacy. For anything else, email the address above.

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority where you live.

Age

You need to be at least 14 to create a Concit account.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
